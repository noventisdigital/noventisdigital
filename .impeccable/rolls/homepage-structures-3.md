# Surface structures, round 3 (homepage, Persuade). Written before the roll on 2026-10-16.

Concept (John's, 16 Oct 2026): three routes, one handler. A visitor arrives with one of three needs (something needs building; AI needs to land; technology needs someone to run it), named as the verbs Build, Land, Run. The same person answers all three. The centrepiece shows the three routes in three notations of the same data, a routing table, a system diagram and a shell, and morphs between them on one clock, each route holding its position while the notation redraws around it. The table is the resting state and the reduced-motion state. Every route in every view is a link to that route's page.

World: fixed. The maker's catalogue recorded in DESIGN.md (studio-grey ground, white keylined plates with captions, one dark log plate, Archivo across widths, the copper-dot lockup). Nothing invented: the three routes are John's three lines of work; the proof behind each is real (the portal and Labs; the method and rules; the career).

Structures, ordered by resonance:

1. The table leads. Header; the headline across the full measure; beneath it the centrepiece plate spans the page: the routing table at rest (Request / Route / Handler, three rows, Byrne on every row), morphing to the diagram and the shell on the clock. Below the fold: the handler (portrait plate, credit line, one paragraph), then the three routes as plates, then Send a note. Primary action: the three rows.
2. The spread. The catalogue's two-column spread kept, 7:5: left, the headline, the lead and the three routes as three lines with links; right, the centrepiece plate, near square, morphing. Below: handler, routes, note. Primary action: the route lines, repeated in the plate.
3. The handler first. The person leads: portrait plate left, headline and credit line right; the centrepiece sits directly beneath as his specification, the three routes reading as rows of it. For the buyer who hires a person, not a product. Below: routes, note.
4. The shell leads. The first viewport is the dark plate at page width: a session that types `noventis build`, `noventis land`, `noventis run` and prints the table and the diagram as its output. The light catalogue resumes below. Engineer-first; boldest.
5. The drawing at page scale. One architecture drawing fills the first viewport: three labelled arrows from the left (the needs) into one box (the handler) and one arrow out (working software), drawn with the exploded view's leader lines and captions; the table and the shell are the drawing's other states. The headline sits in the drawing's title block.
6. The index. A short catalogue index: the headline, then three numbered entries (Build, Land, Run), one line and a link each, set down the left; the centrepiece small in the right margin like a colophon mark, cycling. Quietest.
7. The sequence. Scroll drives the morph instead of a clock: the first viewport shows the table; as the visitor scrolls the table becomes the diagram, then the shell, each state pinned for a screen with that route's copy beside it. The page is the morph.

Rut (kept out): hero plus three equal service cards with icons; a carousel of three finished graphics; the current invented Item 01.
