# Surface brief: credentials deck "Noventis Credentials - Automation"

Scope and mode: a 12-slide 1920 by 1080 deck in the Claude Design `.dc.html` format (inline-styled `<section>` slides inside the deck-stage import), kept at `docs/design/Noventis Credentials - Automation.dc.html` for John to upload to his Claude Design project with the two files in `docs/design/uploads/`. Persuade. Shown in a first meeting or sent as a PDF afterwards. Sibling of the homepage inside the maker's catalogue world chosen on 16 October 2026 and recorded in DESIGN.md.

Audience and job: the same SME owner-manager, after a first call, often reading the PDF on a phone and forwarding it to a business partner. Headlines must carry the story on their own because body copy is 5 to 7px on a phone.

Action: a free call or a note to hello@noventisdigital.co.uk; LinkedIn named on the closing slide. No price, duration, fee model or phone number anywhere (confirmed 15 October 2026: mirror the site).

Proof: Item 01, the placeholder worked example drawn as an exploded quoting tool (flagged "Illustrative item", captioned); Item 02, the client portal screenshot as a plate with a specification; the placeholder portrait on a plate captioned "photo to come, placeholder"; sectors as a list, no client names. Nothing else may be invented.

Constraints: voice rules (no exclamation marks, no em dashes, British spelling, first person); every slide reads at rest (no motion in a deck); author instructions live only in `data-speaker-notes`, never as slide content; the two surfaces share vocabulary (Item 01 and Item 02, the seven parts, the three offers as Parts 1 to 3, the four rules); fonts load from Google Fonts in the helmet because the deck runs inside Claude Design.

Slide list (confirmed structure, unchanged on 16 October 2026): 1 Cover (headline, lead, maker line, Item 01 exploded). 2 Noventis is me (plate text, portrait plate, specification). 3 The promise. 4 A week before and after (three list plates: To do, In progress, Done, each totalling at its foot). 5 What I automate (three plates plus "Also regularly" tags). 6 How it works (the three offers as Parts 1 to 3, the four rules on a plate). 7 Built securely (three plates, captions tied to parts 5 and 6). 8 What I build (four plates plus Item 02, the portal, with its specification). 9 Where I've done the work (sector list plate). 10 Worked example (Item 01 exploded at 1.18 scale, the story, the parts list, the spec strip, the placeholder note). 11 A bit more about me (portrait plate, specification). 12 Let's talk (headline, plate, ink Send a note, contact specification).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each slide is a page of the same maker's catalogue as the website: items with numbers, plates with captions, specification tables, and the first item exploded into its seven parts, so the deck shows software taken apart rather than listing services. It refuses the credentials-deck template of logo wall, two big numbers and a values grid.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited from the homepage's catalogue world: one studio-grey ground (#e1e4e6) with no frame; every object a white plate with a 2px keyline (#aeb4b8 at slide scale), a downward cast shadow and a caption at its foot in tracked capitals (#60666b); ink #141618, soft ink #474c50; the log part the one dark object (#1b1e21); no accent colour, the action and the "Running today" flag inverted to ink. One family across widths, Archivo from Google Fonts: 125% expanded for item and part numbers (the wordmark is the copper-dot NOVENTIS lockup at 100% width); 88% for headlines at 56 to 128px; 100% for prose at 22 to 34px, tables and captions; tabular figures. The slide header is a 96px bar with the wordmark left and a keylined flag right, over a 2px rule; the cover and closing slide carry a foot line.

STORY: The prospect sees the quoting tool taken apart on the cover, meets the maker on slide 2, reads the three offers as three numbered parts on slide 6, sees real software (Item 02) on slide 8, walks the parts list of Item 01 on slide 10 and stops on parts 5 to 7, and ends on an ink plate that tells them what to send.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The cover at 1920 by 1080: the 96px header with the copper-dot NOVENTIS lockup and its descriptor, and a "Credentials" flag; left, the headline "Most AI consultants ship slides. I ship software." at 104px over two lines, the lead at 34px and the maker line with the placeholder portrait at 76px; right, Item 01 as the exploded drawing at 760 by 720 with its item label and "Illustrative item" flag; the foot line carries John Byrne, noventisdigital.co.uk and the email.

FORM: the same maker's catalogue as the homepage, applied as a second surface inside the chosen world; no new roll (the world is settled and the slide structure is precisely specified). seed: none (unavailable)
