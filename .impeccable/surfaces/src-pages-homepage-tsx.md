# Surface brief: homepage (noventisdigital.co.uk)

Scope and mode: the public homepage at `/`, Persuade. Labs and Privacy inherit the terminal and the world. The credentials deck is a sibling surface under the same world and follows the site (its rebuild to the three routes is pending). The portal, the admin console, the contact backend and the privacy text stay untouched.

Audience and job: two buyers. The UK SME owner-manager with a spreadsheet problem, and the managing director of a 50 to 200 person company with nobody senior in technology. Both arrive from LinkedIn or a referral, on a phone or a laptop. They need to recognise their own problem in one line, decide John is credible, and send a note.

Action: Send a note (the form), with email as the fallback. The page states no price, duration or phone number.

Proof: John's name and portrait (his photograph, supplied 17 October 2026, at public/images/john-byrne.png); the client portal as real software running today, with its screenshot and specification; Labs; his career. Nothing invented: the illustrative Item 01, its story and its two figures were retired on 16 October 2026 (John: leading with an invented item was the real problem).

Constraints: brand voice (understated, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, British spelling, first person); self-hosted fonts; no third-party script on the critical path; GitHub Pages static hosting; reduced motion must show every piece of content; monospace only inside the session plate.
